    What the evals caught while building

    RunScoreWhat failed, and the fix
    v153/58Bills with no buyer name passed. "₹4,000" read as no amount. 05/12/2026 not seen as a future date. Online orders told you to "ask the vendor". Organisers of unknown GST status were assumed unregistered.
    v258/58Fixed all five. Then wrote 5 new cases to check I hadn't just fitted the tests.
    v361/63"Rs. 4,000.00" broke the amount parser. A two-digit year (14-03-26) was silently accepted.
    v463/63Fixed both. Unclear dates are now flagged instead of guessed.
